Frequently asked

Is my text sent to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Quick Sanitize uses pure JavaScript rules. Deep Humanizer downloads an AI model once, stores it locally (IndexedDB) and runs offline. Your text never leaves the browser.

Where is the AI model downloaded from?

On the first run of Deep Humanizer the model weights (~900 MB for Qwen 2.5 1.5B) are downloaded from the public Hugging Face CDN (huggingface.co/mlc-ai). It's a one-time static file download — same as loading a JS library from a CDN. Your text is NOT sent — only the model itself is fetched. After download everything runs offline from IndexedDB and the model is never re-downloaded.

What is Burstiness Score?

Burstiness is the variation of sentence lengths in a text — standard deviation of sentence lengths divided by the mean. Humans write with burstiness 0.65–0.85, AI 0.15–0.30. It's one of the strongest AI detection signals in 2026.

How does Quick Sanitize differ from Deep Humanizer?

Quick Sanitize is pure JS rules — replaces AI words, removes openers like "In today's world", breaks tricolons, balances burstiness. Runs instantly. Deep Humanizer downloads a local AI model (~900 MB) and uses Reverse-Prompting: it extracts bare facts, then rebuilds them from scratch in a chosen persona. Higher quality but requires one-time model download and WebGPU.

Will my text pass AI detectors?

Quick Sanitize catches ~70% of cases with simpler detectors. Deep Humanizer truly breaks AI fingerprints because the text is rebuilt from scratch with a different token distribution. No tool guarantees 100% passage. Use ethically.

Does it work on mobile?

Quick Sanitize — yes, on any browser. Deep Humanizer requires WebGPU and ~2 GB free RAM, so realistically works on desktop/laptop only with Chrome 113+, Edge 113+ or Firefox 121+.
